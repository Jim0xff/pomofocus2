# Conventions — redpacket-server

## Tech conventions
- Language: TypeScript (ESM)
- API: GraphQL-first (Apollo Server)
- ORM: TypeORM entities in `src/models`
- Infra helper modules centralized in `src/infra`

## Operational conventions
- Request-level traceability is expected (project ecosystem commonly enforces requestId propagation).
- Business logic lives in `services`, not resolvers.
- External calls (TaskPoint, chain, subgraph, storage) are encapsulated by dedicated clients/services.

## Data/Deletion policy for reuse
When reusing this template in our workflow, enforce platform rule:
- **No hard delete** in business data paths.
- Deletion behavior must be implemented as soft-delete or status transitions.

## Domain conventions observed
- Redpacket identifiers and permit/signature fields are generated by backend and passed to contract calls.
- Domain includes budget/task/chat side models; keep as optional modules when porting.
